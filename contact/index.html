ONE-ON-ONE STRATEGY

Prefer a conversation?
We’d be happy to talk it through.

Do you work with businesses in both the US and UK?+

Yes. Klyro Works serves businesses across the United States and United Kingdom. We adapt content, terminology and digital journeys to your market and arrange collaboration around your team with dual registered entities in London and the US.

Who owns the intellectual property and code of our website?+

You do. Upon full settlement of project milestones, all custom design files, bespoke frontend code, and written assets created specifically for your project transfer 100% to your company. We provide complete code handover with zero vendor lock-in.

How do you ensure GDPR, CCPA, and privacy compliance?+

Every website we deliver incorporates privacy-by-design: explicit consent mechanisms on lead forms, accessible cookie preference centers, data minimization, and transparent privacy disclosures adhering strictly to UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and US CCPA/CPRA regulations.

Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?+

Yes. We routinely execute mutual NDAs prior to in-depth technical audits, proprietary workflow mapping, or reviewing confidential commercial roadmaps.

How much does a project cost and how are payments structured?+

Pricing is scoped transparently based on your deliverables, custom integrations, and complexity. Fixed-scope projects are billed in structured milestones (initial deposit, development milestones, and final launch). We invoice in your local currency (USD $ or GBP £).

Can we start with just one service?+

Absolutely. We frequently begin with a focused website redesign, an SEO technical audit, or a targeted AI lead qualification workflow, scaling up as your business needs grow.

Do you guarantee rankings, leads or revenue?+

No. In alignment with FTC and UK Advertising Standards (CAP Code) rules, we do not make algorithmic or revenue guarantees that depend on third-party search engines or market conditions. We establish measurable KPIs, implement proven strategies, and provide transparent reporting.

What happens after launch?+

Every build includes a 30-day post-launch warranty for bug fixes. We also offer ongoing maintenance, search optimization retainers, and workflow enhancements to support your internal team.
