2. Categories of Information We Collect

We strictly adhere to the principle of data minimization. We only collect information necessary to evaluate your inquiry and provide professional digital services:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: When submitting our consultation form or contacting us, you may share your full name, work email address, company or trading name, target market (US/UK/Global), service interests, and project brief description.
  • Information We Never Request: Our marketing forms strictly prohibit and do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial account numbers, credit cards, government identification (SSN/National Insurance), or protected health records.
  • Automatically Logged Technical Information: When browsing our website, our hosting servers collect standard server logs (including truncated IP addresses, browser user-agent, operating system, requested URL, and timestamps) solely for security diagnostics, DDoS mitigation, and system health monitoring.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (UK & EU GDPR)

Under Article 6 of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, we process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): By explicitly opting in on our consultation form, you consent to our team reviewing your project details and contacting you for an initial consultation. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Pre-Contractual & Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to prepare tailored proposals, scopes of work, client agreements, and to deliver agreed deliverables.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Protecting our web infrastructure against malicious attacks, preventing fraud, and maintaining business records.

We Do Not Sell or Share Your Personal Information

5. United Kingdom & European Data Subject Rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, UK and European visitors have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data via a Subject Access Request (SAR).
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data (“Right to be Forgotten”) where processing is no longer required.
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your personal information.
  • Request data portability in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with the UK supervisory authority:

  • No Intrusive Ad Tracking: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site tracking pixels on this website.

7. Data Retention & International Transfers

Enquiry data is retained for up to twelve (12) months following our last interaction, after which it is securely deleted, unless a formal commercial contract is established (in which case relevant financial and contractual records are retained for six (6) years to comply with statutory UK HMRC and US IRS accounting obligations).

Where personal data is transferred across borders between the UK, US, and EU, we ensure adequate protection through approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.
