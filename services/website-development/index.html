Can you redesign an existing website without losing current Google search rankings?+

Yes. We perform a comprehensive pre-migration SEO audit, mapping all existing URLs, metadata, and high-ranking keywords. We implement strict 301 redirect maps, preserve canonical tags, and audit Google Search Console post-launch to ensure zero organic search visibility loss.

Which platform or technology stack do you recommend for our project?+

We evaluate your team's internal workflow and growth goals. For speed-critical B2B platforms and SaaS, we build with modern React/TypeScript architectures. For editorial content teams requiring daily non-technical publishing, we implement headless WordPress or structured CMS platforms with custom-engineered themes.

How long does a custom business website project typically take?+

A focused commercial business website typically launches in 4 to 6 weeks from kick-off to deployment. We work in transparent, two-week milestones with live staging previews so you have full visibility into progress at every phase.

Who owns the website code and design files after completion?+

You own 100% of all bespoke design files, creative assets, and custom source code upon settlement of the final project milestone. We provide complete Git repository access, deployment guides, and admin credentials with zero ongoing agency license fees.

What kind of post-launch warranty and technical support do you offer?+

Every website build includes our 30-day post-launch warranty, covering defect resolution and performance fine-tuning. Following handover, we offer flexible monthly maintenance retainers for security monitoring, content updates, and ongoing conversion rate optimization.
