ONE-ON-ONE STRATEGY

Prefer a conversation?
We’d be happy to talk it through.

Will AI automation replace our existing customer-facing team?+

No. Our automations are engineered to empower your people, not replace them. We eliminate repetitive administrative chores—such as copying form fields into CRMs, drafting initial confirmation emails, or sorting spam inquiries—so your team can spend their time holding high-value conversations with qualified prospects.

How do you prevent the AI from hallucinating or giving inaccurate information?+

We use strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and structured output validation (JSON schemas). The AI is explicitly constrained to reference only your verified company knowledge base and pricing sheets. If an inquiry falls outside approved parameters, the system automatically routes the query to a human team member with a summary note.

Is our proprietary business data used to train public AI models?+

Never. We exclusively use commercial enterprise API tiers from providers like OpenAI, Google Cloud Vertex, and Anthropic. Under their commercial API terms of service, your data is never used to train public foundational models, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and complies with UK and EU GDPR standards.

Which CRM and software platforms can you connect to?+

We connect with virtually any modern platform that provides an API or webhook interface, including HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, Zapier, Make, and custom SQL/PostgreSQL databases. We can also build custom REST middleware for legacy systems.

What happens if a connected third-party tool experiences an API outage?+

We build resilience into every architecture using local database queues and exponential backoff retry mechanisms. If a CRM or email service temporarily goes offline, our middleware securely buffers the inquiry and retries automatically once the service restores, alerting your team if issues persist.
