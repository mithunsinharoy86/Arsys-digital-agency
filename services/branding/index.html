ONE-ON-ONE STRATEGY

Prefer a conversation?
We’d be happy to talk it through.

Can you refresh our existing brand identity without completely discarding our established equity?+

Absolutely. A brand evolution or refresh retains recognized visual equities—such as familiar brand colors or logomark motifs—while modernizing typography, balancing vector geometry, and engineering clean digital-first guidelines suitable for high-resolution screens and responsive apps.

How many design concepts will you present to our team?+

In Phase 2, we present three distinctly divergent creative directions. Each direction showcases the logomark in context, demonstrating how typography, color, and photography come together across web pages, business cards, and marketing materials so you can make an informed, confident strategic choice.

Will we receive editable presentation templates and social media assets?+

Yes. Every branding package includes editable, branded presentation templates (Google Slides, PowerPoint, or Keynote) and customizable social media card templates in Figma so your team can maintain consistency from day one.

How do font licensing and commercial usage rights work?+

We select fonts that align with your budget and application needs. Where premium commercial typefaces are recommended, we outline the exact desktop and web licensing requirements. Alternatively, we can construct the identity using world-class open-source Google Fonts with zero ongoing font licensing fees.

Who owns the final logos and brand guidelines?+

You own 100% of all bespoke logos, vector logomarks, color palettes, and custom guidelines upon settlement of the final project invoice. We provide complete assignment of all commercial intellectual property rights with zero agency royalties.
