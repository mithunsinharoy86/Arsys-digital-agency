Do you guarantee #1 rankings on Google?+

No ethical or compliant agency can guarantee a #1 ranking. Google’s proprietary ranking algorithms change hundreds of times per year, and third-party search results are outside any agency’s direct control. In strict compliance with US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA/CAP Code), we do not make unsubstantiated ranking promises. Instead, we guarantee rigorous technical compliance, white-hat methodology, and measurable visibility growth.

How long does it typically take to see measurable organic search improvements?+

While technical fixes (such as resolving broken redirects, robots.txt blocks, and indexing errors) can result in improvements within 2 to 4 weeks, strategic keyword rank growth typically requires 3 to 6 months of sustained technical health and content authority. We provide real-time Google Search Console tracking so you observe crawl and impression trends from week one.

How do you handle Local SEO for businesses targeting specific cities in the US or UK?+

We optimize your Google Business Profile (GBP), ensure strict NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across verified regional directories, and implement localized Schema.org markup. For multi-location businesses, we engineer distinct, valuable location landing pages with unique localized service content, strictly avoiding doorway pages or duplicate city templates that violate Google guidelines.

Will you need access to our website source code or CMS?+

Yes. Effective technical SEO requires access to your CMS (e.g. WordPress, Webflow, Next.js repository) and Google Search Console. We can either deploy technical fixes directly through your team’s established Git/staging workflow or provide detailed, annotated pull requests and engineering tickets for your internal developers.

Who owns the research, keyword models, and optimized content?+

You own 100% of all keyword intelligence databases, competitive research files, Schema code, Looker Studio dashboards, and written copy produced during the engagement upon settlement of milestone invoices. We do not retain proprietary rights or lock you into agency-owned software.
