ONE-ON-ONE STRATEGY

Prefer a conversation?
We’d be happy to talk it through.

Should our brand choose standard Shopify Plus or a Headless Commerce architecture?+

For most direct-to-consumer and B2B brands, a bespoke Shopify Plus theme engineered with clean Liquid, Tailwind CSS, and sub-second asset bundling delivers the optimal balance of ultra-fast load speed and non-technical staff usability. For complex brands requiring bespoke custom product customizers, international multi-region microservices, or complex ERP integrations, we engineer headless React/Hydrogen storefronts.

Can you migrate our existing store from WooCommerce, Magento, or older systems?+

Yes. We manage end-to-end migrations including customer account records, order histories, product catalogs, SKU variants, and SEO redirect maps. We implement rigorous 301 URL redirect mappings to protect your organic Google rankings and preserve historic revenue pipelines.

How do you handle multi-currency checkout for US and UK customers?+

We configure localized multi-currency and international markets using Shopify Markets or native multi-region gateways. Customers in the United Kingdom pay in British Pounds (£) with localized VAT handling, while US shoppers checkout in US Dollars ($) with automatic state sales tax calculation.

Will our internal marketing team be able to add new products and edit pages easily?+

Yes. We build using Shopify’s native Online Store 2.0 modular section architecture. Your team can easily reorder content sections, update promotional banners, and launch new product pages without touching a single line of code.

What kind of conversion optimization (CRO) testing do you implement?+

We test high-impact conversion friction points: sticky "Add to Bag" buttons on mobile, express one-click wallet integrations, quantity tier discount bundles, real-time inventory threshold badges, and transparent shipping timeline disclosures that build immediate buying confidence.
