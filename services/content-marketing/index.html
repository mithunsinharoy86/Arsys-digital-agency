ONE-ON-ONE STRATEGY

Prefer a conversation?
We’d be happy to talk it through.

Do you use automated AI tools to write the content?+

No. While we leverage search intelligence tools like Ahrefs and Semrush to uncover commercial search intent, all copywriting is 100% human-crafted by experienced commercial editors. Automated AI tools produce generic, repetitive fluff that Google’s Helpful Content system actively devalues and that fails to persuade sophisticated buyers.

How do you ensure the written content accurately reflects our technical domain?+

We conduct 45-minute discovery interviews with your internal subject-matter experts before writing. We extract your proprietary methodology, client case studies, and specialized terminology, translating your authentic expertise into clear, accessible, and high-converting copy.

How does high-quality content marketing support our Google search rankings?+

Search engines evaluate whether a website demonstrates comprehensive topical authority and satisfies user search intent. By publishing well-structured, in-depth service pages and educational guides with clear Schema markup and internal links, we help Google comprehend your expertise, resulting in higher rankings across broad clusters of commercial keywords.

Can you audit and rewrite our existing website copy instead of starting from scratch?+

Yes. We frequently conduct content audits of existing websites, identifying pages with good search impressions but low click-through or high bounce rates. We restructure headings, clarify the value proposition, inject rich Schema.org markup, and optimize the copy for conversion.

Who owns the copyright to the written articles and copy?+

You own 100% of all written copy, editorial guides, research databases, and meta descriptions upon final milestone payment. We provide complete assignment of all commercial rights, allowing you to publish, syndicate, and repurpose the text across any channel.
