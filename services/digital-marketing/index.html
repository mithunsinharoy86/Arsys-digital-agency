ONE-ON-ONE STRATEGY

Prefer a conversation?
We’d be happy to talk it through.

Do you charge a percentage of our advertising spend or a fixed management fee?+

We operate on a transparent fixed monthly management tier based on account scope and complexity, not a percentage of ad spend. Percentage-of-spend models create a conflict of interest where agencies benefit from you spending more money. Our incentive is strictly to lower your cost-per-acquisition and maximize your return on ad spend (ROAS).

Who owns the Google Ads account and campaign data?+

You own the account 100%. We configure and manage campaigns directly within your own Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising accounts. If our engagement ever concludes, you retain all campaign structures, keyword data, negative lists, and historical analytics with zero disruption.

How much ad spend should our business allocate each month?+

Media budgets depend on your industry’s average cost-per-click and target market. For focused B2B service firms in the US or UK, we typically recommend a minimum starting ad spend of $2,000 / £1,500 per month to generate statistically significant conversion volume. Ad spend is paid directly to Google, completely separate from our agency management fee.

Do you build custom landing pages or send paid traffic to our main website?+

Sending paid traffic to a general homepage usually results in high bounce rates and wasted budget. We engineer dedicated, distraction-free landing pages tailored to the exact search query and pain point of the ad, loading in under 1.2 seconds with clear trust signals and streamlined inquiry forms.

How do you measure lead quality and prevent spam form submissions?+

We implement server-side honeypots, reCAPTCHA v3 invisible validation, and Google Tag Manager enhanced conversion validation. Furthermore, we integrate CRM offline conversion tracking (OCT) so Google’s bidding algorithms optimize for closed deals and qualified opportunities rather than raw, low-value form fills.
