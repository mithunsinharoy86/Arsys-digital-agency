ONE-ON-ONE STRATEGY

Prefer a conversation?
We’d be happy to talk it through.

How much time will our leadership or founder team need to contribute each month?+

We understand your executives are busy running the business. We require just one 45-minute strategic interview per month. Our senior editorial team extracts your unique insights, war stories, and industry perspectives, turning them into a full month of polished, authoritative thought leadership posts and carousels for your review and sign-off.

Which social media channels should our business prioritize?+

For B2B services, technology firms, and commercial consultancies, LinkedIn delivers the highest commercial return on investment by far. For luxury retail, real estate, and consumer brands, Meta (Instagram/Facebook) and YouTube offer powerful visual storytelling. We recommend dominating 1 or 2 high-impact channels rather than spreading resources thin.

How do you measure whether social media is actually driving revenue?+

We implement the LinkedIn Insight Tag, Meta Conversions API, and custom UTM parameter structures tracked directly in your Google Analytics 4 and CRM. We track not just "likes" or vanity impressions, but website visits, content downloads, consultation requests, and closed deals initiated from social touchpoints.

Do you create original video and graphics or use stock templates?+

Every graphic, document carousel, and video snippet is custom-designed in Figma and Premiere Pro using your brand guidelines, custom typography, and real photography. We strictly avoid generic Canva templates or artificial stock photos that erode authority.

Can you handle community management and inbound direct messages (DMs)?+

Yes. We monitor comments and inbound inquiries daily. We provide prompt, professional responses adhering to your brand voice guidelines and escalate qualified commercial prospects directly to your sales team with full context.
